# POTIFY Telegram Editorial Kit marketing and sales plan

**Owner.** Chance owns the public release and approves campaign copy.

**Audience.** Community editors and Telegram maintainers. **Offer.** Give editors an iPhone-friendly way to inspect a Telegram draft and its duplicate history before publication.

| Proposed timing | Public material | Desired outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–3 | Show a duplicate draft caught during a phone-based review and the difference between a draft and a dispatch receipt. | Give the audience one verifiable reason to try the project. |
| Days 4–10 | Share the reviewed demo or sample through the project page and one relevant creator or community channel. | Check a fictional draft locally. |
| Days 11–28 | Publish a build note showing what the trial revealed and one change made from feedback. | 5 successful duplicate-draft reviews. |

### Sales test

Show the review routine to three editorial teams and target one paid setup workshop. Keep any publishing authority in the team’s existing approval system. Validate one paid editorial onboarding session after the team documents a reduction in duplicated work.

### Measurement and decision

Use `telegram-editorial-first-public-build` for the pilot. Record a 28-day prelaunch baseline where available, then observe each participant for seven days after their first visit. Current audience, conversion and net revenue baselines are unknown. The numbers above are proposed targets. Distinguish visits, qualified actions, buyer inquiries and paid receipts. Keep private identifiers in the authorized reporting system. If the outcome cannot be attributed, label it unknown.

Proposed pilot budget is $0 for distribution and two founder hours for the first demonstration. At day 7, inspect the biggest point of friction. At day 28, expand only if the target action was measured and the sales test produced identifiable demand. Report fulfilled sales net of refunds and direct costs. A product serving trust or internal operations may have no direct sales at all.

**Prerequisite for external promotion.** The historical starter describes automated posting. The shipped review tool is offline. Current publishing remains subject to Chance’s approval. Draft copy and placements go through the existing approval process.
